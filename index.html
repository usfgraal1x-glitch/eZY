      Frequently Asked Questions

      Is eZy legit?
      Yes — every order is confirmed and delivered directly by our team, so you always know who you're dealing with.
      How does delivery actually work?
      Once your payment is confirmed, most digital items (Steam cards, UC, etc.) are delivered within 5 minutes. V-Bucks are delivered within 10 minutes after payment is confirmed and the required codes are provided. Physical items (controllers, keyboards, headsets) ship in 1–3 business days with tracking info sent to you directly.
      Why do physical items need a deposit?
      Physical items are sourced from abroad, so we require a deposit to confirm the order. You have two options: pay 30% upfront to reserve your order, or pay the full amount upfront. Orders without at least the 30% deposit can't be confirmed.
      What if my code doesn't work or I don't receive my item?
      Contact us immediately with your order reference. If the issue is on our end — wrong code, missing delivery — we'll fix it or refund you. This is the one exception to our no-refund policy, and we take it seriously.
      Why do I have to pay before you deliver?
      Because digital codes are irreversible once sent — this protects us the same way COD protects you on a physical order. We keep processing near-instant specifically so this doesn't feel like a leap of faith.
      Is my payment information safe?
      We don't store your payment details — you pay directly via InstaPay, Telda, Vodafone Cash or PayPal to our listed accounts, and there's no card data passing through our site.
